Old. Still moving.

Squared is not a finished product. It is a nascent idea with real motion behind it.

We have grown from zero to a paying member community in under a year. We have a fully functional accountability app with squads, dedicated leaders, a goal system, a coin reward system, and a mentorship layer. We have a Facebook community of over 23,000 people. And over 200 people signed up to the Squared Prize waitlist in a matter of days — without a single paid ad.

We are building the biggest platform for ambitious young creatives in Nigeria. That is not a small thing. And we are doing it with almost nothing.

The offer

We can't pay you.
We want to be upfront about that.

Squared is bootstrapped and founder-led. Every naira we make goes back into the product and the community. We are not in a position to hire right now — and we refuse to pretend otherwise.

What we can offer is this:

  • A front-row seat to something being built from scratch.
  • Real responsibility — not busywork.
  • A community that will know your name.
  • The kind of experience that only comes from being early to something that works.

If you are the kind of person who sees value in that — we want to hear from you.

Open roles

What we're
looking for.

Six roles. A few good people. That's all.

We should also mention — being an early volunteer at a growing platform like Squared is exactly the kind of thing that looks good on a CV. Just saying.

🎙️
Community Manager
This is the most important role on this list. The Squared Facebook community has over 23,000 people in it. It needs a warm, consistent human presence — someone who posts, responds, and keeps the energy alive. Not every day. Not a full-time job. Just someone who genuinely enjoys being in the room and wants to help shape what it becomes.
What we need: Someone who writes well in a casual tone. Someone already active in Nigerian online communities. Someone who cares.
📱
Social Media Manager
We have a strong brand voice. We just need someone to help carry it consistently across X, Instagram, and TikTok while the founder is heads-down building the thing. You'll work with existing content direction — we're not asking you to invent everything from scratch.
What we need: Someone who understands how each platform works. An eye for what performs. Basic consistency.
✍️
Content Contributor
Squared has stories worth telling. Member wins, community moments, ideas about what it means to build something in Nigeria. If you like to write and want a real audience for your work — this is for you. Contribute when you can. We'll publish what's good.
What we need: Someone who can write. Really write. Clear, honest, and in a voice that sounds like a person.
🎬
Videographer / Editor
Short-form content. Member spotlights. Behind-the-scenes moments. If you have a phone and basic editing skills, you qualify. We're not looking for a Netflix production — just someone who can make things look intentional.
What we need: Basic shooting and editing skills. Consistency over perfection. A phone works fine.
🛡️
Moderator
As the community grows, someone needs to keep it the kind of place people actually want to be in. Welcome new members. Flag spam. Keep conversations healthy. Low pressure, high impact.
What we need: Someone trustworthy, calm, and fair. Someone who understands what Squared stands for.
🎓
Campus Ambassador
Squared's long-term vision includes physical spaces on Nigerian university campuses. Before we get there, we need people already on the ground — building awareness and representing Squared in their schools. You don't have to do much. Just be the person who people think of when they hear "Squared."
What we need: A current university student in Nigeria. Someone known and respected in their campus community.
